---
title: Customize Without Losing It
description: Edit skills and agents freely — the CLI never silently overwrites your changes.
---

The whole point of the delivery model is that you **customize** the capabilities
for your project. The CLI is built so a later `rvst-cli link` never destroys
your edits.

## How upgrades handle your edits

The CLI records a content hash of every file it installs. On the next `link`
after a new registry version:

- **Files you never touched** update silently to the new version.
- **Files you edited since install** are protected:
  - **Interactive run** — you get a per-file prompt: **keep**, **overwrite**, or
    view a **diff** (a `[d]iff` loop shows exactly what changed upstream vs.
    your version).
  - **Non-interactive run** (e.g. CI) — the CLI keeps your version and warns.
    It never clobbers silently.

This means you can safely tune an agent's tone, tweak a skill's steps, or fill
in a steering file, and know an upgrade won't wipe it out.

## What's safe to edit

- **Steering** — fill in `product.md`, `tech.md`, `structure.md`; adjust
  standards if your project genuinely diverges.
- **Skills** — tune the steps or triggers of any `SKILL.md`.
- **Agents** — adjust an agent's instructions, welcome message, or skill list.

## Recommended workflow

1. Edit the file you want to customize.
2. Keep the change small and purposeful — you'll be re-reading it at each
   upgrade diff.
3. On the next `rvst-cli link`, choose **diff** to see what upstream changed,
   then **keep** or merge deliberately.

:::tip
If you find yourself making the *same* customization in every project, that's a
signal it should go **into the registry** instead. Raise it so everyone gets it.
:::
